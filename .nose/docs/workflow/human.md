# Human

You know the product. You do not need to know software. You do not run a process.

**Talk.** The agent writes every file, chooses the phase, and only asks you about the product.

## You say

- Brain dumps, half-finished thoughts, or a clear idea.
- What “working” looks like. What this must never be.
- What might change in your world later.
- Optional: “this should be a change axis because …”
- Yes / no / later / never to recaps and to bets you did not name.
- Mind changes in ordinary language (“also Slack”, “we will never do X”).
- At the end: accept the product, or say what is wrong.

## You answer, in your language

Questions like: could A happen without B? how often, 1–5? another of the same kind or a different kind? now, later, or off the table? same program or a separate product?

You do not have to know what an API protocol is. If the agent infers something, they explain it as a bet in your words and wait.

## You do not

- Pick phases, edit yaml, name modules, or read scores.
- Confirm extra copies in the code. That is the agent’s job.
